# Pasta with mushrooms

## Ingredients
- Button Mushrooms
- Onions
- Garlic
- Butter/Oil
- vegtable broth
- Cream
- Pasta

## Cook
- cut mushrooms and fry them **without** oil, this releases the water and increases the Umami flavour
- once nicely brown add chopped onions and garlic to the pan
- let them brown for 3 minutes
- deglace with broth and add cream to your liking, let it simmer until the consistance is nice
- coook the pasta aldente in the meantime

## Enjoy
